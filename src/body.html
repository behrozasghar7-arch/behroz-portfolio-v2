02 / About

A designer by profession. A creative problem-solver by nature.

I started my creative journey in 2018 as a graphic designer, working on social media visuals, brand identities and marketing materials.

Since then I've worked with businesses, agencies and organisations in real estate, hospitality, education, technology and digital marketing.

What started as graphic design grew into something bigger: understanding how brands communicate and how visuals can solve real business problems.

Today I create brand identities, websites and motion-driven visuals. I'm also exploring AI-assisted development with Claude, ChatGPT and VS Code.

06 / Design Meets Technology

From pixels to possibilities.

I began with traditional graphic design tools: identities, campaigns and visual content. Today I combine Figma and Adobe Creative Suite with AI-assisted development.

With VS Code, ChatGPT and Claude, I turn visual concepts into interactive websites and prototypes. I'm especially drawn to SaaS animation, interactive interfaces and workflows that bring design and development closer together.

The goal isn't to replace creativity with technology. It's to use technology to make creative ideas possible.
